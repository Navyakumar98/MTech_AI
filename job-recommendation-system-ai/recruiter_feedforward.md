The feed-forward process begins by cleaning the input job description and extracting deterministic features such as required skills and minimum years of experience. Next, the system uses a pre-trained SentenceTransformer model to generate normalized semantic embeddings for both the job description and the candidate resumes. A base score is then calculated for each candidate by combining their semantic similarity to the job description, computed via FAISS, with their skill and experience match scores. When recruiter feedback is utilized, the system constructs a user profile vector from previously rated candidates and adjusts the base scores to favor candidates semantically similar to those who received high ratings. Finally, the candidates are sorted by their final computed scores to produce the top-K recommendations, while offline metrics like NDCG and Precision are calculated to evaluate the ranking quality.
